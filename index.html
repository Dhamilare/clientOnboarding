How onboarding works

One form starts four things happening on your behalf.

01

Contact details, work experience, and how you learn best.

02

Your answer is reviewed to determine whether you learn best visually, by listening, by reading, or by doing.

03

A private folder and welcome packet are generated for our placement team.

04

You hear from us

A confirmation email arrives with next steps and resources matched to how you learn.
