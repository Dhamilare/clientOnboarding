How onboarding works

One form. Four things happen automatically.

From the moment you submit, your case file, training match, and welcome packet are already in motion.

01

Share your background

Contact details, skills, and the kind of work you're seeking.

02

AI identifies your learning style

Your answer is analyzed to see whether you learn best visually, by listening, by reading, or by doing.

03

Your case file is created

A private folder and welcome packet are generated for our placement team.

04

You hear from us

A confirmation email arrives with next steps and resources matched to how you learn.
